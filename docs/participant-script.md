# Participant Read-Aloud Script

> This operational script must be checked against the study's approved ethics/IRB materials before data collection. It supplements the approved participant information sheet; it does not replace it.

“Thank you for taking part. You will solve nine tangram puzzles in three short sittings of three puzzles each. We will take a short break after puzzle three and puzzle six.

Each puzzle uses all seven tangram pieces, and every piece must be used in the final shape. Each designated starting spot has a dot that marks the center of its piece. Whenever you are not using a piece—even briefly—or you are unsure what to do, please put it back so the center of the piece sits over its matching dot. This is very important: the robot can only pick up pieces from those marked starting spots.

The amount and timing of assistance may vary. During some puzzles you may receive an on-screen hint, hear an alert, or see the robot move a piece. During other puzzles you may not receive assistance. Please work naturally; we are evaluating the system, not testing you.

A timer and the current round number will remain visible while each puzzle is active. One high beep marks the start of the puzzle, two slower beeps mark the midpoint, and a different low sound marks the end of the planned puzzle time. A bright two-beep alert means that a questionnaire is ready. The researcher can pause and resume the timer if needed. Additional sounds indicate a new hint or robot movement.

The researcher controls robot movements through fixed numbered programs: 1 Orange Triangle, 2 Green Square, 3 Red Triangle, 4 Pink Triangle, 5 Yellow Parallelogram, 6 Blue Triangle, and 7 Purple Triangle. You do not need to operate these controls yourself.

After every puzzle, please complete the short questionnaire on your screen. The researcher’s screen will show when the questionnaire has been filled. After all nine puzzles, there is one final questionnaire and an optional comment box.

Before we begin, please enter your age and gender selection, rate how effective you expect the robot assistance to be, and review the consent statement. You can ask questions now or at any point. Taking part is voluntary, and you may ask to pause or stop at any time without penalty.”

## Confirmations shown in the app

- “I have read and understood the study instructions.”
- “I consent to participate in this study.”
