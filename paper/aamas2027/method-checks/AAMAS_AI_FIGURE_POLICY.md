# AAMAS 2027 AI and figure policy check

Checked 7 October 2026 against the official submission instructions:
https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/aamas2027/guidelines-and-policies/instructions/

The current policy permits AI-assisted text polishing/formatting and creation of code/scripts for proofs of concept, demonstrations and experiments. AI use for hypotheses or methods, including experimental design, requires detailed disclosure in the paper or supplement, including prompt, tool and version. Authors remain accountable for accuracy and citations.

AI-generated images/multimedia are permitted only when generative AI is the research topic and the media serve as qualitative research evidence. The policy does not explicitly define whether diagrams rendered from AI-assisted TikZ code are within that restriction. It does not provide a blanket LaTeX exception.

Our setup/architecture figures are deterministic vector schematics from editable TikZ, reflecting author-supplied layout/protocol and checked code. The plots are deterministic data charts; the setup photograph is author-supplied. No image-generation model was used. These technical facts distinguish their provenance, but do not establish a policy exemption. Treat the TikZ interpretation as unresolved until clarified by the conference. No contact was sent on the authors’ behalf.

The analysis choices and discussion were developed with AI assistance during revision. Before submission the human authors must review methodology provenance and determine the required supplementary disclosure under the official rule; the brief manuscript disclosure alone is not proof of compliance. Do not claim the system experimentally used generative AI or alter provenance to evade the policy.

Formatting: at most eight main-text pages plus unrestricted bibliography pages; class/layout must not be altered to squeeze the paper. The new clean draft’s main text ends on page 8, with references continuing on page 9.
