# Supplementary analysis details

These notes accompany the descriptive results in the working manuscript. They preserve scoring and missing-data details without interrupting the paper's main narrative. The final supplement must be updated with the final dataset and analysis decisions.

## Workload scoring and aggregation

All six NASA-TLX dimension items use response values from 1 to 7. The performance item increases from failure to perfect performance; the other five increase with demand, effort or frustration. The overall workload score is the unweighted mean of mental demand, physical demand, temporal demand, `8 - performance`, effort and frustration. Individual performance scores in the table retain their original direction. This reporting uses seven-point NASA-TLX items and does not apply pairwise dimension weights.

For ratings and round durations, three trials are first averaged within each participant and condition. The displayed mean and sample standard deviation are then computed across the nine participant averages. The summary script checks the composite against each completed trial. The analysis is descriptive, with no inferential test or significance claim.

Round duration is the exported pause-adjusted duration across all attempts, including unsolved puzzles. Some durations exceed the nominal five-minute allocation; they have not been truncated. The final analysis needs a documented rule for timeouts, late completion and timing deviations. Robot-cue counts identify issued cues, rather than independently measured movement onset or completion.

## Completion coding and sensitivity

One participant lacks recorded binary completion labels. The bundle's working reconstruction identifies two scheduled trials as solved; a final adaptive trial is ambiguous. The primary comparison reports 4/27 control, 19/27 scheduled and 13/27 adaptive successes. Excluding this participant gives 4/24, 17/24 and 13/24. Counting the ambiguous adaptive trial as solved gives 14/27 adaptive successes. These alternatives retain the ordering of the condition-level completion rates.

A separate partial session contains three completed control trials and two completed adaptive trials, with one adaptive success. Including it yields 4/30, 19/27 and 14/29 solved trials. Its unfinished and unstarted trials are missing outcomes, not failures. The main comparison uses the nine complete participants to retain three trials per condition for each person.

## Remaining analyses

Condition-order counts and puzzle assignments differ within the current subset; adaptive trials do not include puzzles 2 or 3. Scheduled trials contain 270 text hints and 54 robot cues; adaptive trials contain 230 hints and 31 cues. These differences matter when interpreting the policies. Final analysis should account for repeated participants and consider puzzle identity, block position, missingness and assistance exposure.

Five participants provided written comments, but no qualitative coding is reported. The independent task-profile survey and correct-piece photograph scoring remain pending. The single end-of-study trust and reliance items describe the overall interaction and cannot be retrospectively separated by condition.
