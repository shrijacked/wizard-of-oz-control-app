# Supplementary AI-use statement

This statement accompanies the brief disclosure in the manuscript. It records the assistance known from the writing task; it does not make claims about undocumented AI use earlier in the project.

## Tool and scope

OpenAI Codex assisted with literature organization and checking, manuscript drafting and revision, LaTeX preparation, and code for descriptive summaries. The session identified the model family as GPT-6; an exact model/version identifier was not retained in this document. It should not be reconstructed or guessed.

The study's three conditions, task and intended sample were supplied by the research team. Codex organized the existing aims into research questions, suggested discussion and future-work wording, and implemented descriptive calculations. The calculations average repeated ratings and durations within participant and condition, summarize them across participants, and report completion frequencies and coding sensitivities. The participant records and existing outcome coding supply the data; the tool did not collect observations or generate replacement participant outcomes.

The researchers clarified that constant assistance is offered every 30 seconds and adaptive assistance whenever the existing dashboard arousal flag is raised. This clarification supersedes an earlier draft's interpretation of the available software controls as a discretionary intervention policy. The software's arousal flag uses heart-rate change; it is not a separate increase-in-HRV threshold.

## Retained prompt excerpts and interaction

The interaction was an extended writing conversation. These verbatim excerpts identify the relevant requests; they are not represented as the complete conversation:

> okay, start writing it. also document the flow somewhere so it can be picked upon using other people working on it too.

> draft this as an actual paper for now, well replace the findings later on

> make sure the references are verified by downloading the pdfs and going through paper text - we dont want hallucinated references or data. also we are trying to use most of the papers from within the last 2-3 years

> can you map every reference to its text in the corresponding pdf? also explain what structure is being followed currently

These requests led to a full working manuscript, a descriptive-summary script, reference verification against downloaded PDFs, and a citation-to-passage map. Subsequent author feedback supplied the working abstract, corrected the assistance timing policy, and requested a consistent author voice with analysis provenance kept outside the main narrative. The source conversation remains the fuller record; this statement is a summary and does not reproduce all prompts or generated responses. Any AI contribution to the original study design outside this writing task still needs to be documented by the team if applicable.

## Policy and responsibility

The [AAMAS 2027 instructions](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/aamas2027/guidelines-and-policies/instructions/) permit AI assistance for text polishing/formatting and code. For AI involvement in hypotheses or methodology, the policy requires details including prompts and tool/version in the paper or supplementary material. The [conference Q&A](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/aamas2027/guidelines-and-policies/qa/) allows an accurate account when exact records are incomplete and says not to invent missing information. The information above includes substantive drafting and analytical assistance rather than characterizing the interaction as proofreading only.

The authors are responsible for the accuracy, originality and integrity of the submitted work and for appropriate citation. Before submission, this record should be reconciled with the retained conversation and any earlier AI use. Local author notes and raw records are working materials, not automatically submission supplements.
