# Baseline-recording check

Checked 7 October 2026. Internal guidance review, not a claim about the enacted procedure or an added manuscript reference.

The author confirmed on 7 October that the three-minute pre-task window after study information is the actual procedure. The current collector instead sets BASELINE_DURATION=60.0 and computes baseline HR over that window. A three-minute preparatory/recording period and the actual software reference interval are distinct. The manuscript retains that confirmed procedure and removes the confirmation reminder. The software reference interval remains a separate implementation detail; no software settings were changed.

[Laborde, Mosley and Thayer 2017](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.00213) was downloaded as a PDF and the relevant duration/baseline text on PDF pages 8–9 read directly. PDF page 8 recommends five-minute recordings when possible for comparable HRV estimates, with shorter recordings depending on the measure and research question. PDF page 9 recommends consistent baseline posture/instructions and an acclimatization period, illustrating that recording length alone does not establish a suitable baseline. These HRV recommendations do not validate this study's HR-rise detector or its 5-bpm threshold.

For a mean-HR reference, the available evidence does not establish that the author's three-minute window is invalid; it also does not establish that the actual recording was stable or standardized. Report what was done rather than retroactively changing its duration to match a recommendation. Do not turn a provisional preparation window into an asserted validated resting-HRV protocol.
