# Questionnaire inventory

Verbatim wording from `public/subject.js` and `public/subject.html` at modi5 commit cd1aa9d. All response scales are integers 1–7. This records the implemented questionnaire, not proof of the version administered to every participant.

| Item | 1 | 7 |
|---|---|---|
| Before starting, how effective do you expect the robot assistance to be? | Not effective at all | Extremely effective |

| Stored key | Exact item | 1 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| helpfulness | Helpfulness: How helpful did you find the text hints and the robotic arm's movements in solving the tangram puzzle? | Not helpful at all (I completely ignored or did not need the assistance) | Extremely helpful (I could not have progressed without the assistance) |

| stressReduction | Stress Reduction: When you found yourself stuck, how effective were the interventions at reducing your frustration? | Not effective at all (my frustration remained the same or increased) | Highly effective (they completely relieved my frustration) |

The final questionnaire also offers an optional written comment (stored up to 4,000 characters). Demographics, instruction acknowledgement and consent are collected at onboarding. The `automationBias` label does not establish a validated scale or observed automation bias.

## Recruitment questionnaire

Read on 5 October 2026 from the user-supplied Google Form; no form was submitted and no respondent data were accessed.

[Participant Recruitment: Human Robot Tangram Study](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeUBuL_EK9pZhZSXmWdSUQ63j-0UZ89Iu-Q3PbGnT-8aq0WLg/viewform)

The introduction describes nine timed puzzles across three sittings, possible text and robot assistance, wrist sensing, workspace recording, and questionnaires. Fields:

- Email, full name, phone number, age, gender.
- Current occupation or academic role: undergraduate, master’s, PhD, research staff/researcher, TA, professor/faculty, other.
- Previous participation in a similar experiment: yes / no / maybe.
- Previous experience interacting with robots: none / a little / some / a lot / prefer not to say.
- Willingness to record the puzzle workspace, with the statement that the face will not be recorded: yes / no.
- General availability.
- Consent to participate: yes / no.

These questions support recruitment and profiling. They do not supply findings about task choice, preferred help timing, or the merits of a particular assistance strategy. Actual form responses and procedures are needed to report sample characteristics, eligibility decisions, and recording coverage.
