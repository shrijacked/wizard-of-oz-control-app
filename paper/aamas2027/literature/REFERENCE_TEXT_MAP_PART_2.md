## Publication filter and changes to the earlier shortlist

Selection requires a verifiable peer-reviewed journal publication or an established conference paper, plus a readable full text and a direct connection to an existing subsection. IROS, IEEE RO-MAN and ACM THRI provide the clearest robotics venue anchors. IJSR, JMIR and Cognitive Research provide relevant journal evidence. The three Frontiers articles are published, peer-reviewed specialist-journal studies; they are not represented as flagship conference papers or assigned an unsupported ranking. See the [journal peer-review policy](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/about).

Nine previously identified papers remain after this publication check. Singh et al., *Neuroadaptation in Physical Human-Robot Collaboration* (arXiv:2310.00351), is excluded because no journal or conference publication could be verified; Cavicchi et al. replaces it. The short HRI Companion initiative paper is also not selected. Download location alone is not publication status: the IROS and RO-MAN author manuscripts have independent conference evidence. For RO-MAN 2026, presentation is verified, but an IEEE archival DOI is not yet verified.
